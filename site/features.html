01 · Gameplay

Systems that give the world a pulse

Enough structure to make progress matter, without turning survival into a job.

🛒

Player economy

An in-game currency that circulates between players, not into a shop menu. Open a chest shop, price your goods, and let the market decide. Balances persist across restarts and are visible in the control room.

🔨

Auction house

List rare items and bid against other players. Good for enchanted gear, bulk materials, and anything a chest shop is too clunky for.

🏦

Loans & interest

Short on capital for a big build? Borrow against future earnings and repay on a schedule. Keeps ambitious projects from stalling.

🏘️

Towns & claims

Found a town, invite members, and claim the land around it. Claims are protected automatically — no command gymnastics required.

⛏️

Progression

Playtime and milestones unlock cosmetics and perks. Nothing pay-to-win — everything is earned in the world.

🎣

Custom content

Server-only items, recipes, and world events that make this world different from every other survival map.

02 · Crossplay

Everyone in the same world

Geyser and Floodgate translate Bedrock clients into the Java server, so there is one world, one economy, and one chat — not two parallel servers.

Java

Desktop, macOS, Linux

cairo-paste.tun.ply.gg:25565

Works with vanilla, OptiFine, Fabric, and most mods that don't change world generation.

Bedrock

Phone, console, Windows

fried-toilette.tun.ply.gg:37298

iOS, Android, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch, and the Windows Bedrock app all connect with the same address.

Both editions share inventory-free survival: your progress, claims, and money follow your account, so you can switch devices without losing your place.

04 · Tools

Built for the people running it

Owners get a real operations surface instead of squinting at a console log.

🎛️

Control room

A password-protected web panel that shows who is online, world state, and the economy at a glance. It sits behind its own login with per-session CSRF protection and rate limiting, and its public address is refreshed automatically.

05 · Fair play

Rules with teeth

Moderation that is consistent, logged, and open to appeal.

🛡️

Anti-cheat

Movement, combat, and inventory checks flag impossible behaviour for review.

↩️

Rollback

Griefed builds can be restored from block history, sometimes without a restart.

⚖️

Appeals

Every action is logged, and there is a clear path to appeal a decision.

🧑‍⚖️

Real people

Decisions are made by the owners, not an opaque automatic system.
