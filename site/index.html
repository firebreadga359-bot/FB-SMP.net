Why it lasts

Built to be logged into for years

Not a throwaway server. Every system is designed so the world keeps going whether or not anyone is watching it.

🛰️

Runs itself

A watchdog restarts anything that dies, keeps the tunnels open, and pages the owners when something is wrong — so downtime is measured in seconds.

📱

True crossplay

Java and Bedrock share one world through Geyser and Floodgate. Phone, console, and desktop players all meet in the same place.

🛒

A living economy

Player shops, an auction house, and loans. Specialise, trade, and build wealth that carries across sessions.

🧱

Builds that stay built

Land claims, rollback, and active moderation mean griefers can't undo your work.
