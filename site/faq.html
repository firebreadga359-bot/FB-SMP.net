Joining

Getting in

Is the server free to join?
Yes. There is nothing to buy and no paid ranks that affect gameplay. Progress is earned in the world.
Do I need to be whitelisted?
Access is invite-based so the world stays a known community. Use the sign-up form and you will get join details back.
What version do I need?
The current release of Minecraft. Java and Bedrock are both supported, and the server accepts a small range of versions around the latest.
Can I play on console?
Yes — Xbox, PlayStation, and Switch connect through the Bedrock address. Some consoles hide the server list until you add a server once from a phone or the Windows app.
Are mods allowed?
Client-side quality-of-life mods are fine. Anything that gives an unfair advantage — x-ray, auto-mining, flight — is cheating. See the rules.

Gameplay

In the world

Is there a shared economy?
Yes. A player-run currency circulates through chest shops, an auction house, and loans. You can ignore it and play pure survival, or lean in and specialise.
Will my build be protected?
Claimed land is protected automatically, and unclaimed builds are still covered by the no-griefing rule. Rollback can restore damage from block history.
Do I lose progress when the server restarts?
No. Restarts are clean and the world persists. Your inventory, claims, and balance survive.
Can I invite friends?
Yes — have them use the sign-up form. Mention who referred them and it is usually quick.

The infrastructure

How it stays online

What does "24/7" actually mean here?
A watchdog restarts anything that stops responding, a scheduled keep-alive re-opens the play tunnels, and external checks alert the owners within minutes if something is unreachable. See status.
Why is the address a long hostname?
The world is reached through a tunnel rather than a fixed IP, so the address is a hostname with a port. Copy it exactly as shown on the join page.
Who runs this?
A small group of owners, with the build, automation, and this site largely written by an AI agent on OpenHands. The project is public on GitHub.
How do I report a bug or a player?
Open an issue in the repository. Bugs get fixed; reports get investigated against the logs.
