Fair play

Rules, kept
short and clear

These exist so that other people's builds and time are safe. Break them and you lose access — there is no grey area, but there is an appeal.

The rules

Ten things that matter

🚫

No griefing

Do not break, burn, flood, or bury anything you did not build. Claims are protected, but unclaimed builds are covered by this rule too.

🧰

No stealing

Chests, farms, and animals belong to whoever made them. Ask before you take.

⚙️

No cheating

No hacked clients, x-ray, fly, auto-mine, or anything that does the game for you. Quality-of-life mods are fine.

🕳️

No exploits

Duplication, lag machines, and abusing bugs for money or items are treated as cheating. Report a bug and you may be rewarded instead.

💬

Respect people

No harassment, slurs, or targeting. Banter is fine; cruelty is not.

🔞

Keep it appropriate

This is an all-ages server. No sexual content, no shock builds, no spam.

📢

No advertising

Do not recruit for other servers or sell anything for real money.

🤝

Honour trades

Agree a price, complete the trade. Scamming is a bannable offence.

🧑‍⚖️

Follow staff

If a moderator asks you to stop something, stop. Argue it in an appeal, not in chat.

🙋

Ask first

Unsure whether something is allowed? Ask before you do it — that is always the safe path.

Second chances

Appeals

If you believe an action was wrong, you can appeal it. Read the process before you send one.

How do I appeal a ban?
Open an issue in the project repository with the label appeal, including your in-game name, what happened, and why you think the decision was wrong. Keep it factual — you are talking to the people who made the call.
How long does an appeal take?
Usually a few days. Temporary bans run their course regardless, so appealing early is in your interest.
Can I appeal a friend's ban?
Only the banned player should appeal, but others can add context to the same issue.
What if I was banned for something I didn't do?
Say so plainly and include anything that helps: timestamps, witnesses, screenshots. Actions are logged, so claims can be checked against records.
